Meet ImageGlass at WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2025 — Open Source Spotlight

We're excited to share that ImageGlass has been selected to participate in the Open Source Spotlight at this year's WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2025 in Berlin!

The Open Source Spotlight is a dedicated space at WeAreDevelopers that celebrates open-source projects making a positive impact. We're honored to be among those invited — and especially proud to share this moment in ImageGlass's 15th anniversary year.

How to Find Us

The event is hosted in Berlin's state-of-the-art convention center in the Westend / Charlottenburg district.

  • 📍 Location: Messe Berlin, South Entrance & CityCube
  • 🏙️ District: Westend / Charlottenburg, Berlin, Germany
  • 📅 Date & Time: July 10-11, 2025 08:00 - 18:00
  • 🌐 Event Website: https://www.wearedevelopers.com/world-congress

If You're Attending…

Come visit us in the Open Source Spotlight area! We'll be there to:

  • Say hello and hand out some free stickers 🎁
  • Demo the latest ImageGlass features, and share advanced tips (especially for enterprise users)
  • Talk about the project's 15-year journey and some stories you may not have heard

Whether you're new to ImageGlass or have been with us since the early days, we'd love to meet you.

Meet the creator of ImageGlass

This is a rare chance to chat face-to-face with the developer behind the project. If you want a quiet or casual conversation while we're in Europe, feel free to get in touch here — we'd be happy to connect.

Thank You!

To everyone who has downloaded, contributed, donated, reported a bug, translated a string, or simply told a friend about ImageGlass — thank you.

We're proud to still be here after 15 years, and grateful to be part of this open-source community. This spotlight is not just about the software, but about all of you who have helped shape and support it along the way.

Here's to the journey so far — and to what's next.
