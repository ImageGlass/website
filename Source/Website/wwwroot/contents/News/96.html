ImageGlass Joins OpenPledge.io

We are excited to share that ImageGlass is now available on OpenPledge.io — a platform that helps open-source contributors get rewarded for fixing issues and building new features.

With OpenPledge, you can:

  • Pledge funds toward specific ImageGlass issues or feature requests
  • Work on pledged issues and earn rewards once your pull request is merged

This helps make ImageGlass development more sustainable and community-driven. Check out ImageGlass on OpenPledge to get involved.

Thank you for supporting open-source development!
