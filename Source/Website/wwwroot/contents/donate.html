Support ImageGlass

ImageGlass is feature-rich, simple-to-use, open-source software designed for seamless viewing of images in a clean, modern and intuitive interface. Built with care by a one-man team and supported by a passionate community, ImageGlass is completely free of ads, trackers, or locked features — and I intend to keep it that way.

If you find ImageGlass useful and would like to support its ongoing development, consider making a donation. Your contributions help cover:

  • Development time and long-term maintenance
  • Hosting and infrastructure costs
  • Licensing for essential development tools
  • Testing and quality assurance
  • New features, improvements, and bug fixes

Partnership

We're open to partnerships with sponsors, developers, and the community to make ImageGlass even better. Whether it's through sponsorship, tech integration, or collaboration, we'd love to hear from you – contact us to explore partnership opportunities.
