# ImageGlass Joins OpenPledge.io

We are excited to share that ImageGlass is now available on [OpenPledge.io](https://openpledge.io) — a platform that helps open-source contributors get rewarded for fixing issues and building new features.

<img alt="ImageGlass joins OpenPledge" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/064ecab6-97a8-4b07-80a6-b27c8d1e049b" />


With OpenPledge, you can:
- Pledge funds toward specific ImageGlass issues or feature requests
- Work on pledged issues and earn rewards once your pull request is merged

This helps make ImageGlass development more sustainable and community-driven. Check out [ImageGlass on OpenPledge](https://app.openpledge.io/repositories/d2phap/ImageGlass) to get involved.

Thank you for supporting open-source development!
